02 / A little context

A polymath's curiosity.
A builder's instinct.

There is always something
worth understanding better.

I like following a problem far enough to understand what it actually needs. Sometimes that means writing an API. Sometimes it means rethinking an interface, checking a targeting rule, or connecting a CRM to the process around it.

My foundation is full-stack engineering. Working across GTM systems, Salesforce, and security training gives me different ways to look at the same problem.

Kaizen keeps me moving. Notice the friction. Make a useful change. Learn from what happens. That can be a cleaner component, a better qualification rule, or documentation that makes the next person's job easier.

Education, community platforms, and accessible public services are areas I care about. CampusHub and my work on Focused Network reflect that interest.
