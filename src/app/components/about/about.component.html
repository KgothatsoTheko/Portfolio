A polymath's curiosity.
A builder's instinct.

There is always something
worth understanding better.

I like getting to the root of a problem instead of just fixing the part I can see. Sometimes that means writing an API. Other times, it means changing an interface, fixing a bad targeting rule, or connecting a CRM to the rest of the workflow.

I started with full-stack software engineering, but over time I’ve worked across GTM systems, Salesforce, automation, and cybersecurity too. I like that mix because it lets me look at a problem from more than one angle.

Kaizen keeps me moving. Find something that could be better. Fix it. See what happens. Then improve it again. That might be cleaner/organized code, a better qualification rule, or preparing documentation that makes the next person's job easier.

I’m also interested in building things that are genuinely useful and impactful. Education, community platforms, and accessible public services are a few areas I keep coming back to. Projects like SA-Digital, CampusHub and my work on Focused Network come from that.
